Cranberry salad

Make 11/25 and refrigerate

Ingredients

  • 1 pound fresh cranberries
  • 1 can (20 oz) crushed pineapple, well drained
  • 2 boxes cherry jello
  • 1 box orange jello
  • 1½ cups sugar
  • 1 cup chopped pecans
  • 1 cup chopped celery
  • 1 navel orange and its zest
  • 1 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Chop the cranberries. Add ½ cup sugar and stir.
  2. Boil 2 cups water. In a large bowl, mix the jello, 1 cup sugar, and 1 tsp salt. Add the boiling water and stir well.
  3. Add the cranberries, pineapple, pecans, celery, chopped orange, and zest. Stir everything together.
  4. Pour into a pan and refrigerate.
