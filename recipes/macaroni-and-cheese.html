Macaroni and cheese

Make at 1:00 PM; bake 3:30 PM at 350°F

Ingredients

  • 4 cups dried macaroni
  • 1 whole egg
  • ¼ cup (½ stick) salted butter
  • ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  • 2½ cups whole milk
  • 2 tsp (heaping) dry mustard
  • 1 lb cheese (cheddar, jack, and/or fontina), grated
  • ½ tsp salt, more to taste
  • ½ tsp seasoned salt, more to taste
  • ½ tsp ground black pepper
  • ¼ tsp cayenne pepper

Instructions

  1. Cook the macaroni until very firm: too firm to eat right out of the pot. Drain.
  2. Beat the egg in a small bowl.
  3. In a large pot, melt the butter and sprinkle in the flour. Whisk over medium-low heat for 5 minutes, constantly. Don’t let it burn.
  4. Pour in the milk, add the mustard, and whisk until smooth. Cook 5 minutes until very thick. Reduce heat to low.
  5. Slowly pour ¼ cup of the sauce into the beaten egg, whisking constantly. Whisk smooth, then pour the egg mixture back into the sauce, stirring until smooth.
  6. Add the cheese and stir to melt. Add the salt, seasoned salt, and pepper. Taste and add more. DO NOT UNDERSALT.
  7. Stir in the macaroni. Serve right away (very creamy), or pour into a buttered baking dish, top with extra cheese, and bake at 350°F for 20 to 25 minutes, until bubbly and golden.
