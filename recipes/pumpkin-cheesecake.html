Pumpkin cheesecake

Make 11/25; bakes 1 hour, then chill 4+ hours

Ingredients

  • Crust: 1¾ cups graham cracker crumbs
  • Crust: 3 tablespoons light brown sugar
  • Crust: ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • Crust: 1 stick melted butter
  • Filling: 3 (8 oz) packages cream cheese, room temp
  • Filling: 1 (15 oz) can pureed pumpkin
  • Filling: 3 eggs plus 1 yolk
  • Filling: ¼ cup sour cream
  • Filling: 1½ cups sugar
  • Filling: ½ tsp ground cinnamon
  • Filling: ⅛ tsp ground nutmeg
  • Filling: ⅛ tsp ground cloves
  • Filling: 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • Filling: 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Crust: combine crumbs, sugar, and cinnamon in a medium bowl. Add the melted butter. Press flat into a 9-inch springform pan and set aside.
  3. Filling: beat the cream cheese until smooth. Add the pumpkin, eggs, yolk, sour cream, sugar, and spices. Add the flour and vanilla and beat until well combined.
  4. Pour into the crust and spread evenly. Bake 1 hour.
  5. Remove and let sit 15 minutes. Cover with plastic wrap and refrigerate at least 4 hours.
