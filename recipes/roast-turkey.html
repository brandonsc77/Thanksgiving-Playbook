Roast turkey

14–16 lb bird: 2 to 2½ hours total

Ingredients

  • 1 red apple, sliced
  • ½ onion, sliced
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 cup water
  • 4 sprigs rosemary
  • 6 leaves sage
  • Canola oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 500°F. Remove the bird from the brine, rinse inside and out with cold water, and discard the brine.
  2. Place the bird on a roasting rack inside a half sheet pan and pat dry.
  3. Microwave the apple, onion, cinnamon stick, and 1 cup water on high for 5 minutes. Put the steeped aromatics in the cavity with the rosemary and sage.
  4. Tuck the wings under and coat the skin liberally with canola oil.
  5. Roast on the lowest oven rack at 500°F for 30 minutes. Cover with the foil turkey triangle, insert a probe thermometer in the thickest part of the breast, and reduce the oven to 350°F. Set the alarm to 161°F.
  6. Rest loosely covered with foil or a large bowl for 15 minutes before carving.
