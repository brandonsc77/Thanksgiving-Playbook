Turkey gravy

Start at noon; simmers while the turkey roasts

Ingredients

  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter (½ stick)
  • Neck and giblets from the turkey (discard the liver)
  • 1 medium onion, sliced
  • 8 cups chicken broth
  • Couple sprigs fresh herbs (thyme, parsley, rosemary, or sage)
  • 1 bay leaf
  • Turkey pan drippings
  • ½ cup all-purpose flour
  • Dash Worcestershire sauce
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium-low heat. Add the neck, giblets, and onion; cook, stirring occasionally, until browned, about 15 minutes.
  2. Add the broth, herbs, and bay leaf. Cover and simmer over medium-low for about 2 hours. Discard the giblets or chop them to add to the gravy.
  3. Pour the pan drippings into a measuring cup and let the fat separate (the freezer helps). Ladle off ½ cup of fat into a saucepan.
  4. Heat the fat over medium. Scatter the flour on top and stir constantly until slightly browned and toasty, about 4 minutes.
  5. Switch to a whisk. Gradually ladle in the hot broth, whisking constantly. Bring to a boil, then simmer gently.
  6. Skim excess fat from the remaining drippings and add the pan juices. Simmer, whisking occasionally, until thickened, about 10 minutes.
  7. Add chopped giblets if desired. Season with Worcestershire, salt, and pepper.
