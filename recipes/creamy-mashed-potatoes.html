Creamy mashed potatoes

Cook at 2:00 PM; oven at 3:30 PM, 350°F

Ingredients

  • 5 pounds Yukon gold or russet potatoes
  • 1½ sticks softened butter, plus more for baking
  • 1½ (8-ounce) packages cream cheese, softened
  • ½ cup half-and-half
  • ½ cup cream
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • Milk, if needed, for thinning

Instructions

  1. Peel the potatoes, rinse, and chop into fourths. Cover with water in a large pot, bring to a boil, and cook until fork tender, 20 to 25 minutes.
  2. Drain and return to the pan. On low heat, mash with a potato masher for about 5 minutes. More steam released is better. Turn off the burner.
  3. Add the butter, cream cheese, half-and-half, cream, and seasonings. Stir to combine; thin with milk if needed. Check the seasoning.
  4. Pour into a large casserole pan and dot the surface with butter. Cover with foil and bake at 350°F for 15 minutes. Remove the foil and bake 10 more minutes. Serve immediately.
