Anna Mae’s dressing

Prep bread and cornbread 2 days ahead; bake 3:00 PM

Ingredients

  • 2 loaves white bread
  • 1 bundle celery hearts
  • 5 large onions
  • 1 stick butter
  • 6 cans chicken broth
  • 12 eggs
  • 3 tsp salt
  • 3 Tbsp dried sage
  • 3 tsp poultry seasoning
  • 1 tin of cornbread

Instructions

  1. Two days ahead: toast both loaves and tear apart. Bake a tin of cornbread and crumble.
  2. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  3. Chop the celery hearts and onions. Sauté in the stick of butter, add chicken broth, and keep cooking.
  4. Season the crumbled bread well with salt, pepper, sage, and poultry seasoning.
  5. Whisk the 12 eggs and pour half into the bread. Mix well.
  6. Add the sautéed veggies to the bread mix. Keep adding broth to the veggies to warm them, then add the warm broth to the bread. Season as you go. You should use about 6 cans. Mix until you get the right consistency.
  7. Start the oven at 400°F, bring it down to 350°F, and bake 45 minutes to 1 hour.
