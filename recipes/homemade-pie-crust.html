Homemade pie crust

Chill the dough at least 1 hour · Makes 2 crusts · Prep 20 min

Ingredients

  • 2½ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 Tbsp sugar (optional, but great for sweet pies)
  • 1 cup (2 sticks) very cold butter, cubed
  • 6 to 8 Tbsp ice water

Instructions

  1. Mix the flour, salt, and sugar.
  2. Cut in the butter with a pastry cutter or your hands. Stop when you have pea-sized chunks. Visible butter chunks are good.
  3. Add the ice water 1 Tbsp at a time. Mix just until the dough comes together. Don’t overwork it.
  4. Divide into 2 discs, wrap, and refrigerate at least 1 hour.
  5. On a lightly floured surface, roll to about ⅛ inch thick and place in the pie dish.
  6. Tips: cold butter makes flaky layers, and not overmixing keeps the crust tender.
  7. Chill everything again if it starts getting warm.
