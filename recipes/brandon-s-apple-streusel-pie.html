Brandon’s apple streusel pie

Bake 400°F, 40 to 50 minutes; rest at least 2 hours · 8 servings · Prep 30 min · Cook 50 min

Ingredients

  • Crust: 2 pie shells (store-bought, or the homemade crust recipe)
  • Filling: 5 Honeycrisp apples (or a mix of Honeycrisp and Granny Smith)
  • Filling: ⅓ cup sugar
  • Filling: ⅓ cup light brown sugar
  • Filling: 3 Tbsp flour
  • Filling: 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Filling: ¼ tsp nutmeg
  • Filling: 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Filling: 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Filling: pinch of salt
  • Filling: ½ stick butter, cubed
  • Streusel: 1 cup flour
  • Streusel: ½ cup brown sugar
  • Streusel: 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Streusel: ¼ cup old-fashioned oats
  • Streusel: pinch of salt
  • Streusel: 1 stick cold butter, cubed

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Peel and thinly slice the apples. Toss with the lemon juice first.
  3. Combine the sugars, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt.
  4. Add the apples and vanilla and toss well.
  5. Let sit 10 to 15 minutes to pull the juices out.
  6. Pour the filling and all the juices into the crust. Dot with the cubed butter. Optional: a tiny drizzle of honey over the apples.
  7. Streusel: mix the flour, brown sugar, cinnamon, oats, and salt.
  8. Cut in the cold butter until crumbly with pea-sized chunks. Sprinkle a thick layer on top.
  9. Optional: a little extra brown sugar on top for a caramelized crunch.
  10. Cover the crust edges with foil. Bake 40 to 50 minutes, removing the foil halfway through, until the top is golden brown and the filling is bubbling.
  11. Let the pie rest at least 2 hours for clean slices.
