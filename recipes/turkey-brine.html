Turkey brine

Start 3 days before: thaw the turkey · Makes 1 turkey (14 to 16 lb) · Prep 20 min · Cook 10 min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup kosher salt
  • ½ cup light brown sugar
  • 1 gallon vegetable stock
  • 1 tablespoon black peppercorns
  • 1½ teaspoons allspice berries
  • 1½ teaspoons chopped candied ginger
  • 1 gallon heavily iced water

Instructions

  1. Combine stock, salt, brown sugar, peppercorns, allspice, and ginger in a large stockpot over medium-high heat.
  2. Stir occasionally to dissolve and bring to a boil.
  3. Remove from heat, cool to room temperature, and refrigerate.
  4. Two nights before Thanksgiving, combine the brine, water, and ice in the 5-gallon bucket.
  5. Place the thawed turkey (innards removed) breast side down in the brine.
  6. Weigh it down if needed so it is fully submerged.
  7. Cover and refrigerate or keep in a cool area.
  8. Turn the bird once, halfway through.
